Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Simple Summary: Sleep Coach does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device. We respect your privacy completely.

Information Collection

Sleep Coach does not collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics from users. We do not:

  • Collect your name, email, or contact information
  • Track your app usage or breathing sessions
  • Gather location data
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Collect device identifiers or advertising IDs

Data Storage

All app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Your voice preference (male or female)
  • Your selected breathing pattern
  • Any app customization settings

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

Sleep Coach does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. The app functions completely independently and offline.

Internet Connection

Sleep Coach works entirely offline. No internet connection is required after installation, and the app does not communicate with any external servers.

Children's Privacy

Sleep Coach does not knowingly collect any information from children or adults. The app is designed to be completely private and does not collect data from users of any age.

Data Security

Since Sleep Coach does not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your information. Your privacy is protected by design.

Your Rights

Because we don't collect any data about you, there is no data to:

  • Request access to
  • Request deletion of
  • Request correction of
  • Export or transfer

You maintain complete control over your device and any local app settings.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this policy and notify users through an app update. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

We are committed to maintaining a privacy-first approach and will never introduce data collection without clear user consent and transparency.
