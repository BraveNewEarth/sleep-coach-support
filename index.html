Sleep Coach

Voice-Guided Breathing for Better Sleep

About Sleep Coach

Sleep Coach is a simple, audio-first breathing app designed to help you fall asleep naturally. With soothing voice guidance and two proven breathing patterns, you can ease into rest without complexity or subscriptions.

How to Use

  1. Choose Your Pattern: Select Beginner (4-6-8) or Advanced (5-7-8)
  2. Select Your Voice: Tap the Mode button to choose male or female voice guidance
  3. Start Your Session: Press "Start Session" and close your eyes
  4. Follow the Voice: Let the calming audio guide you through 8 breathing cycles
  5. Drift to Sleep: When the gong sounds, it's time to rest

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need an internet connection?
No! Sleep Coach works completely offline once installed. Perfect for airplane mode or areas without service.
Q: Which pattern should I start with?
Start with Beginner (4-6-8). It's gentler and easier to follow if you're new to breathwork. Once comfortable, try Advanced (5-7-8) for deeper relaxation.
Q: I feel lightheaded. Is this normal?
Yes, this is common when starting breathwork. Your body is adjusting to a new oxygen/CO₂ rhythm. Start seated with back support, and if it persists, try the Beginner pattern or consult a healthcare professional.
Q: Can I use this lying down?
Yes, but start seated first until the practice feels effortless. Once comfortable, lying down is perfect for sleep sessions.
Q: How do I change the voice?
Tap the "Mode" button (gear icon) on the home screen. You can choose between female and male voice guidance.
Q: Is there a subscription?
No! Sleep Coach is a one-time purchase. No subscriptions, no recurring fees, no upsells.
Q: Does this replace medical treatment?
No. Sleep Coach is a support tool to help calm your body, but it's not a replacement for medical care or treatment for anxiety, insomnia, or other health conditions.

Important Safety Information

  • Go Easy at First: Lightheadedness is normal when starting breathwork
  • Sit First, Lie Later: Begin seated until the practice feels effortless
  • Health Check: If you have lung, heart, or complex mental health conditions, consult a professional before use
  • Keep the Ratio: The inhale:hold:exhale ratio matters more than exact timing
  • Not a Cure: This is a support tool, not a replacement for medical care
